1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Pastel Pals: Arena Rush (the “Game”), a desktop game distributed for Windows. It describes the information the Game handles and the limited circumstances in which your device may contact third-party services.

The Game is designed to keep gameplay progress and preferences on your device. Based on the current version (1.0.0), it does not include a player account system, online multiplayer, advertising, in-game purchases, or integrated analytics.

In short: your game progress and settings are stored locally on your computer. The developer does not receive that locally stored game data through the Game.

2. Information the Game uses

The Game may create and read the following information on your device so that it can function:

  • Gameplay progress, including the current or saved run.
  • Your best score and in-game currency/progression.
  • Unlocked or selected cosmetic items.
  • Game preferences, such as graphics, controls, language, and sensitivity settings.

The current version does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other account details. It does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or camera data.

Your computer and operating system may independently create ordinary technical logs or crash reports. Those are controlled by your operating system and are not the Game's own player analytics system.

3. Where information is stored

Gameplay information and preferences are stored in your browser-based local storage on the device where you play. This allows the Game to remember progress and settings between sessions.

That information is not uploaded to the developer by the Game. It may be lost if you clear the Game's site/app data, reset your browser storage, uninstall the Game, or otherwise remove the relevant local files. Moving to another computer will not automatically transfer your progress.

4. How information is shared

The developer does not sell, rent, or share your locally stored gameplay data. The current version has no built-in account, advertising, analytics, or online multiplayer service that sends gameplay data to the developer.

Information may still be handled by third parties as described below when your device requests externally hosted resources.

5. Third-party services and internet requests

The current Windows build references Google Fonts for typography and includes a fallback request to the Three.js library hosted by cdnjs if the locally bundled Three.js library is unavailable. When a device requests a resource from an external provider, that provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser/app request details, and the time of the request. The provider's own privacy policy applies to its handling of that request.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy (cdnjs is hosted by Cloudflare)

The Game does not send your saved scores, currency, settings, or saved run to these providers as part of those resource requests. Third-party services may change their practices, so please review their policies for current details.

6. Your choices and control

  • You can stop using the Game at any time.
  • You can remove locally stored progress by using any reset option provided in the Game, or by clearing the Game's local app/site data through your device.
  • You can restrict internet access through your device or network settings. Some externally hosted resources may then fail to load.

Clearing local data may permanently remove saved progress and settings.

7. Children's privacy

The Game does not require an account and the current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided to the developer in connection with the Game, please contact the developer using the details below so the concern can be reviewed.

8. Security

Keeping progress on your own device reduces the amount of gameplay information transmitted by the Game. However, no software or method of electronic storage is completely secure. Please keep your device and operating system up to date and protect access to your computer.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Game changes or when legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the policy was most recently revised. If a future version adds accounts, online play, analytics, advertising, or other data practices, this policy should be updated before those features are released.
